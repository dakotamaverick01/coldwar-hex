## Main description (paste into "Description")

### Autumn Forge

**A cold dawn on the Central Front, 1985.**

Autumn Forge is a turn-based wargame about a hypothetical Soviet attack on West Germany in the last years of the Cold War. You command NATO battalions on a living 3D landscape: mist in the valleys, low gold light, wet earth, and villages you can see from above.

**The first battlefield is the Hof Gap** in northeast Bavaria, a real approach route that NATO planners worried about. More battles are planned, so the name covers the whole series, not one map.

### What it will be
- Turn-based, solo against a computer opponent
- Operational scale: each unit is a battalion, on a hex map
- A real 3D landscape with the hex grid hidden until you need it
- Made for phones first, playable in a browser

### Where it stands today
This is an **early prototype**, and I'm posting it early on purpose so people can follow along. Right now the project is working out its look: the lighting, mist and terrain. Playable units and rules come next. Expect rough edges, placeholder shapes and big changes between versions.

### An experiment in building with AI
This is a hobby project and a learning exercise. I'm building it with **Claude** (an AI assistant made by Anthropic), with me making the design decisions and Claude doing much of the coding and planning. I'm learning game development as I go, and I'll share what works and what doesn't in the devlog.

### About the setting
Autumn Forge is a work of fiction and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NATO or any government or military. The name nods to the real "Autumn Forge" exercise series, but the game and its scenarios are original. The war it depicts never happened.
