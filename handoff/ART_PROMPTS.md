## 1. Key art for the itch.io page
A wide painted illustration, tabletop strategy game key art, viewed from a high angle like a miniature diorama. A small Franconian village in northern Bavaria in October 1985: white plastered half-timbered farmhouses with dark brown timber frames and steep red-brown tile roofs, plank barns, a white church with a dark slate spire, chimney smoke drifting. A narrow river with a stone road bridge runs through the middle. Long strip fields in brown, ochre and green, spruce forests on rolling hills, a few orange and yellow autumn trees. Three olive-drab 1980s American M60 tanks and two M113 armoured carriers wait on the near bank beside the village, crews in the hatches. Clear morning light, low sun from the left, long soft shadows, light haze in the distance. Calm and tense, no fighting yet. Painterly but grounded, muted natural colours, no text, no logos, no flags, 16:9.
